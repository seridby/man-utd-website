How it started

The club was founded in 1878 as Newton Heath LYR, a team of railway workers in Manchester. In 1902 it was saved from financial trouble and renamed Manchester United.

Key moments

  1. 1878: the club is founded as Newton Heath LYR
  2. 1902: the name changes to Manchester United
  3. 1910: the club moves to Old Trafford
  4. 1958: the Munich air disaster, a tragedy that shook the football world
  5. 1968: United win their first European Cup
  6. 1986: Sir Alex Ferguson becomes manager
  7. 1999: United win the treble: the league, the FA Cup and the Champions League
  8. 2008: United win the Champions League again
  9. 2013: Sir Alex Ferguson retires after 26 years

Honours

United have won a record 20 English league titles and three European Cups (1968, 1999 and 2008).
